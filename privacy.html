RigRout Privacy Policy

Effective July 16, 2026

RigRout is a commercial-route planning and road-information application. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Location

RigRout requests approximate or precise location only when you choose a GPS feature, such as setting your origin, centering the map, or attaching a location to a hazard report. Location is used to provide that feature. RigRout does not request background location.

Information you submit

If you submit feedback or a community hazard report, the message and any location you include are sent to the RigRout server. Hazard reports are shared with other users and expire automatically. Do not include personal or confidential information in a public hazard report.

Saved information

Vehicle settings, preferences, saved routes, and queued submissions may be stored locally on your device. The service also keeps limited server logs needed for security, reliability, abuse prevention, and troubleshooting.

Third-party services

RigRout uses mapping, routing, traffic, weather, and public transportation-data providers to deliver its features. Requests may disclose ordinary technical information such as IP address and requested map area to those providers. Their own privacy policies govern their processing.

Sharing and sale

RigRout does not sell personal information. Information is shared only as needed to operate the app, comply with law, protect the service, or display a community report you deliberately submit.

Retention and deletion

Local app information can be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Community hazards expire automatically. To request deletion of submitted feedback or other server-held information, use the Feedback feature in the app and identify the submission as precisely as possible.

Children

RigRout is intended for commercial drivers and dispatch operations, not children under 13.

Security and changes

Reasonable safeguards are used, but no network service is completely secure. This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change; the effective date above will be revised.

Contact

Privacy questions and requests can be sent through the in-app Feedback feature.
