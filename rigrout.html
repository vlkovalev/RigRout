Informational Use Only

RigRout is a routing assistance tool intended to support — not replace — the professional judgment of commercial vehicle operators. All route data, road ban feeds, and stop information are for informational purposes only.

No Guarantee of Accuracy

Road conditions, bridge clearances, weight restrictions, and seasonal bans may be incomplete, delayed, or inaccurate. Truck routing is powered by TomTom where available, with an OpenStreetMap/OSRM preview as a fallback; map tiles are provided by Esri, OpenStreetMap, CARTO, or OpenTopoMap depending on the selected map style; restriction data comes from provincial/state 511 feeds. None of these sources may reflect current ground conditions.

Driver & Operator Responsibility

  • You are solely responsible for complying with all posted signs, weight limits, bridge clearances, permit requirements, and applicable laws.
  • Always verify restrictions with the relevant DOT or provincial authority before dispatch.
  • Routing suggestions do not constitute legal, regulatory, or safety advice.

Limitation of Liability

RigRout and its developers accept no liability for accidents, fines, permit violations, cargo damage, delays, or any losses arising from use of this application. Use at your own risk.
